# Checkpoint 0: Migration dataset report

## Scope and migration boundary

This report documents the version `v0.1` migration mapping for all **55** records in the legacy `window.SCHOLARSHIPS` array. It is a data-preservation exercise, not a claim that any opportunity fact is current or correct.

- Audit date: **2026-07-12**
- Total legacy records found and mapped: **55**
- Legacy IDs: integers **1 through 55**, each present exactly once
- Production database changes: **none**
- Production publication approval: **none**
- Internet verification performed: **none**
- Initial verification state for every record: `not-reverified`
- Initial `lastCheckedAt` for every record: `null`

All 55 records require verification against an official source before publication. A syntactically valid URL, an `exact` legacy precision value, or a 2026 date does not make a record verified.

The counts in this report were calculated programmatically from `data.js`. `app.js` and `README.txt` were inspected read-only to understand field semantics, countdown behaviour, and the legacy guide's verification warning. No production migration or database import was performed as part of this report.

## Migration limitations and risks

1. **No official re-verification:** the migration is derived only from the local prototype and its guide-based summaries. No title, provider, deadline, benefit, eligibility rule, status, or URL has been checked online.
2. **Default opportunity type:** all rows are initially labelled `scholarship` as required, although the legacy catalogue appears to mix scholarships with fellowships, tuition policies, mobility support, doctoral networks, and research grants. This default must not become an unreviewed permanent classification.
3. **No structured providers:** all 55 provider names are null. Titles cannot safely establish provider identity, administrative responsibility, or official domain ownership.
4. **Stale-cycle exposure:** all stored dates are in 2026, 30 dated rows are already fully past on the audit date, and the model has no verified recurrence rule.
5. **Legacy precision is not verification:** even the 24 `exact` rows lack an official source check and check date. Estimated dates are precise-looking proxies for broad text.
6. **Deadline semantics are incomplete:** there is no opening date, deadline type, applicant versus nomination distinction, intake, programme, audience, recurrence, timezone, or official call identifier.
7. **Geography is shallow:** country/region values preserve catalogue labels only. They do not distinguish host location, provider jurisdiction, nationality eligibility, consortium coverage, or current participation.
8. **Study taxonomy is mixed:** `Research`, `Postdoc`, and `Exchange` remain in `studyLevels` for fidelity but need a future audience/career-stage model.
9. **Benefits and eligibility are prose:** amounts, currencies, cadence, tuition coverage, nationality, residence, discipline, age, experience, and admission requirements cannot be safely queried or compared without reviewed structuring.
10. **One URL per record is insufficient provenance:** a general information page may not support every stored fact or the operative cycle. There is no field-level source history.
11. **URL structure is not live validity:** the 55 strings parse as HTTPS URLs, but redirects, dead links, ownership changes, superseded pages, and content relevance remain unknown.
12. **Legacy identity is local:** sequential IDs and generated slugs provide migration traceability but do not establish durable cross-cycle opportunity identity or deduplication across renamed schemes.
13. **No change history:** the prototype cannot represent corrections, superseded calls, discontinued schemes, or prior verification decisions.
14. **No production import yet:** this versioned seed is a review artifact. It must remain separate from user tracking data and must not be treated as a production database.

## Recommended official-verification sequence

1. Review the **41 urgent** rows first, keeping the 11 undated and 30 fully past-date reasons visible; then review the remaining 14 rows.
2. For each row, confirm that the opportunity still exists, determine its correct opportunity type, and identify the provider or administering organisation from an official source.
3. Resolve the preserved URL, confirm official ownership, capture the canonical opportunity or current-call URL, and retain redirects or supersession notes rather than silently replacing provenance.
4. Verify the active cycle and application route. Record opening dates, applicant and nomination deadlines separately, timezone, recurrence or rolling semantics, intake, and programme-specific variations.
5. Check benefit and eligibility statements field by field against the relevant official cycle documents. Do not carry forward amounts or requirements merely because the page is reachable.
6. Review location and study-level classifications. Add structured host countries, region coverage, nationality eligibility, career stage, exchange mode, and research audience only when supported.
7. Record the official source, verification outcome, verifier, and real check timestamp at the same time as any approved fact change. Only then move a row out of `not-reverified`.
8. If a scheme is renamed, unavailable, or superseded, retain the legacy row and traceability, record the disposition, and link the replacement instead of deleting history.
9. Run schema and dataset validation again after every reviewed change, including exact record count, unique legacy IDs and slugs, URL syntax, date consistency, and duplicate-URL warnings.

No row should be exposed as a verified production opportunity until this review is complete for that row.
